A law firm's own count of District Court sentences for breach of the primary duty of care (section 36 of the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015) as of April 2018: fines totalling $2.175 million, an average of $145,033.60 per section 36 offence, from $0 (twice) to $380,000, from sentencing outcomes the firm knew of for 14 further WorkSafe prosecutions after the first decision (Budget Plastics). It does not say how the cases were chosen or break them down by sector, and the period is the Act's first two years.
