The credit a business claims back per litre of fuel, by date of purchase. For liquid fuels used in off-road
equipment ('all other business uses') the rate is 52.6 cents a litre for fuel acquired from 2 February to
31 March 2026 (Table 2) and 20.6 cents a litre from 1 April to 30 June 2026 (Table 1), after the page's
note on a 60.9% reduction in fuel excise duty from 1 April 2026. It is a tax rate in cents per litre,
not a cost share of a contract.
