A Queensland Government taskforce of police, QBCC and prosecution officers, led by John Byrne, that
investigated subcontractor non-payment and received 146 submissions complaining of non-payment in 2019.

Reported non-payment ranged from $1,500 to $112 million (the latter one submission about many creditors);
the largest individual loss was $6 million. Excluding submissions relating to multiple creditors, the
average loss per subcontractor was $288,000. About 72% of submissions came from South Queensland, and 90%
of known contractors who made submissions had maximum revenue under $12 million a year.
