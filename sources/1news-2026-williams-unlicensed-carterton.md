A news report on an MBIE prosecution in the Masterton District Court. Jason Williams pleaded guilty to one Building Act 2004 charge for restricted building work between May and December 2022 without a licence or LBP supervision, and was fined $5,600 on 14 August 2026, with $1,000 emotional harm reparation and $130 court costs. The work later needed significant remedial repairs. A press account, not the court record.
