A national study of the cost and benefit-cost record of Australian megaprojects. It describes disputes on single
projects, for example the West Gate Tunnel in Victoria, where the builders claimed PFAS soil contamination was
unforeseeable, against an estimated $1 billion of extra cost (the report's wording, footnoted to another source).
These are one-project accounts. The report does not measure dispute causes, counts or outcomes
across projects.
