The 2025 edition. It reports that 68% of surveyed organisations saw non-labour input costs for infrastructure
projects rise over the last twelve months, and that 36% think prices have not yet peaked (survey of
perceptions, not measured prices). It also says there is no consistent project-level data on subcontracting
and self-performance, that only 8% of firms reported measuring rework as part of productivity metrics (last
year's survey), and that benchmarks such as budgeted against actual cost and indirect labour costs are
something a proposed workstream would first have to collect.
