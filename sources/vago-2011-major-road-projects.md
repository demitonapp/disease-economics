An audit of how VicRoads and the Linking Melbourne Authority managed six major road projects and the
Peninsula Link PPP. Figure 2C gives planned and actual capital cost for four VicRoads design-and-construct
projects: Calder Freeway +4%, Deer Park Bypass +9%, Goulburn Valley Highway 0% and Taylor's Road 0%, three
per cent over approved budget on average. The audit attributes the larger deviations to land prices and
geotechnical conditions the budget had not fully allowed for. It also reports that the public sector
comparator cost for Peninsula Link was revised up by 45% (from $590 million to $858 million) before
the PPP bids were compared. Victoria only, a handful of projects, owner-side budgets.
