A questionnaire survey of Singapore building contractors. The abstract reports average insured,
uninsured and total accident costs of 0.15%, 0.1% and 0.25% of a project's contract sum, and an
insured-to-uninsured cost ratio of 1.5:1 for building projects.

Read from the paper's own abstract, not the full text. The sample size is not in the abstract; a
figure of 117 contractors appears in third-party listings but has not been confirmed from the paper.
