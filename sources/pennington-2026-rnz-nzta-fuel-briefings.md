A news report of the Transport Agency's March 2026 briefings on the effect of diesel and bitumen price rises on its
work. As reported, contractors on projects under a year old are exposed to fuel price changes because index changes in
that first 12 months are not compensated, diesel is seven percent of the road construction index, and bitumen rose
almost 75 percent in three weeks to 20 March. The Agency's early modelling is reported to expect a five percent rise
in big project costs at US$100 a barrel and ten percent at US$150. It is a press account of documents not read here, and
the modelled rises are the Agency's owner-side projection.
