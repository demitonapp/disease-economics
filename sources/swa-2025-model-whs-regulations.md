A national model for each jurisdiction to enact in its own law; it is not itself in force, and the document
points readers to their own jurisdiction's laws. Regulation 39 requires suitable and adequate information,
training and instruction. Regulation 81 requires a high risk work licence for a class of high risk work.
Regulation 246 requires listed items of plant to be registered. Regulation 425 requires an asbestos register
at the workplace. The offences carry a "tier" of monetary penalty, such as tier E and tier G, and the
regulations do not state a dollar amount. Regulation 81 itself carries no penalty line; the offence sits in
section 43 of the Act.
