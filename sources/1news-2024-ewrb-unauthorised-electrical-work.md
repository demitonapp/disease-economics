A news report on six Electrical Workers Registration Board prosecutions under the Electricity Act 1992 heard between March and July 2024, in six district courts. The stated fines are $6,000, $3,500, $6,000, $3,250, $1,200 and $9,000 (the last on a company that knowingly let an unlicensed person install a ventilation system, plus unquantified costs), $28,950 in all. Five concern unauthorised work and one negligent installation. A press account relaying the Board, not its records; the Board's own prosecution page publishes no amounts.
