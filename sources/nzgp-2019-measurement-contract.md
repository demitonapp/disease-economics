Government guidance on re-measurement contracts, which it says suit civil work such as roading with major
excavation where the scope is defined but quantities cannot be fixed until the work is done. The initial sum
is tendered rates times an approximate schedule of quantities, then adjusted as actual quantities are
measured. The sheet says the client carries financial risk because the full cost is not known until
re-measurement. It gives no figure for how far final quantities differ from scheduled ones.
