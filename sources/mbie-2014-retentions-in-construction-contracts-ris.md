The impact statement MBIE signed on 21 November 2014 for the first retention trust proposal (later the 2017 retention money regime). It rests on an October 2014 online survey of the commercial construction sector: 125 usable responses, which MBIE puts at about 13 per cent of the commercial construction market.

It estimates NZ$150 million to NZ$250 million of retentions held in the commercial construction sector, and that payees usually write off NZ$7 million to NZ$16 million of retentions a year, either because pursuing them is not worth it or because the payer is insolvent. A third of payees do not usually write off any; 40 per cent write off 5 per cent or more of their retentions a year on average. MBIE's footnote says significant uncertainty surrounds the statistics because of survey design and extrapolation from the survey to the whole sector.

It is a share of retentions held, not of contract value: the corpus has no denominator for it, so it is a measured loss that cannot be filed as a figure yet.
