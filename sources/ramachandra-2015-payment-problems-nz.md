Online questionnaire of New Zealand practitioners (60 consultants, 15 head contractors and 40 subcontractors responded;
110 to 114 answered each item) who rated 28 causes of payment delays and losses on a 5-point importance scale. Disputes over payment claims and
responses scored a mean of 3.88, second to cash flow difficulties from non-payment on other projects (4.01). Time
overrun of projects scored 3.56 and disputes over quality of work 3.53. The ratings are perceptions of importance, not money, and say nothing about
variation, latent-condition or close-out disputes as such.
