A New Zealand law firm's survey of the construction industry (respondents: employers and principals 30%, main
contractors 22%, project managers 12%, engineers to the contract 8%, subcontractors 4%, others 24%; typical contract
value up to NZ$10m for 41%). The report gives shares of respondents, not a respondent count.

Asked what they expect to cause disputes over the next year or so, respondents named poor quality documentation
(27%), delays in work and extensions of time (20%), issues with quality or inexperience (19%), financial issues
including slow payment (19%), under-pricing at tendering (14%), variations (13%), unfair risk allocation (9%) and
coordination and communication (9%). Respondents saw employer variations as a leading cause of project delay (p.4, a ranking chart with no percentages). The
report holds no dollar amounts for any dispute cause.
