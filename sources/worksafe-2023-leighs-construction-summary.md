The principal contractor in the same 22 February 2019 beam collapse as Culham Engineering, charged under sections 34(1) and 34(2)(b) of the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015 (a duty on a person who engages another). Manukau District Court, 28 November 2023: starting point $18,000, final fine $10,000, costs $1,664.09. No plant registration or certification element.
