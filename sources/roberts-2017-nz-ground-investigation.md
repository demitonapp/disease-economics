A paper to the 20th NZGS Geotechnical Symposium by practitioners and an MBIE author, arguing for a standard ground investigation specification. It says unforeseen ground conditions often cause construction cost over-runs and links them to under-spending on ground investigation. Its evidence for that link is a UK highways study (Mott MacDonald and Soil Mechanics, 1994) and a UK table of site investigation spend as a share of project cost (Table 1, adapted from ICE, 1991).

Nothing in it measures what a New Zealand contractor loses on ground conditions it did not claim for, and its numbers are not New Zealand's.
