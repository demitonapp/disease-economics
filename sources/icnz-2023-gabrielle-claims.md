Insurer-reported claims from Cyclone Gabrielle as at 20 March 2023: 40,275 claims worth about NZ$890 million, of which the commercial line was 6,762 claims and about NZ$352.5 million.

These are insured losses to property and business, not a contractor's unrecovered delay costs, wet days or liquidated damages, and the release does not say whether contract works cover is in the commercial line.
