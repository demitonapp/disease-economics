The Queensland Audit Office's annual review of the state's capital program. "In 2023-24, estimated
actual expenditures were 13.8 per cent higher than budgeted, primarily due to the Transport and Main Roads
portfolio", against a 16.4% variance in 2022-23; since 2022-23 the average is about 15%. It also reports
project-level budget changes: Cross River Rail A$237 million over its original budget at 30 June 2024; Gold
Coast Light Rail Stage 3 from A$1.219 billion to A$1.549 billion; CopperString from A$5 billion to A$6.2
billion.

The previous year's report (Major projects 2023) gave the 2022-23 variance as 14%; this report restates it
as 16.4%.
