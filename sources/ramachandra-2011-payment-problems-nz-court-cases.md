Analysis of 40 payment-related cases filed in the New Zealand High Court in 2008-2010 and of liquidators' reports
for 65 companies. Of the 40 cases, 22 (55%) concerned progress payments, 10 (25%) final payments and 8 (20%) other
payments including retention and costs; 46% of claims were under $100,000 and 11% over $1,000,000. In 16 cases
(40%) the claimant was paid in full after delay and in 10 (25%) the claimant lost. The cases are payment disputes
only, and the study does not split them by variation, delay or ground conditions.
