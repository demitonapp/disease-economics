A painter who subcontracted another painter to paint a roof did not confirm the man was competent or qualified to use the harness he supplied; the subcontractor fell 3.18 metres and died (15 June 2020). Charged under sections 44, 36(1), 48(1) and 48(2)(b) of the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015. Manukau District Court, 27 July 2022: starting point $100,000 reduced to $50,000, with no fine imposed because the defendant could not pay; reparation $100,000 emotional harm. Competence is one strand of a risk-assessment failure, and no fine was in fact imposed.
