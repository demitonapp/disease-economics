A literature and stakeholder scoping study (questionnaires and workshops) of construction and demolition waste in New Zealand. It puts construction and demolition waste at about 40% of landfill, notes that estimates range from 30% to 50% depending on the data source, and lists over-ordering of wet concrete among the sources of concrete waste. Everything is by mass or volume of waste; none is a cost as a share of project value.
