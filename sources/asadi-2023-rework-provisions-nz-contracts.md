Tests how well the New Zealand standard construction contract (NZS 3910:2013) addresses a literature list of rework causes, using a clause review, a Likert questionnaire (81 respondents, a 21% response, 80 completed, client and contractor sides roughly even) and interviews. Five causes score as adequately addressed (Table 3, means 3.39 to 3.83): changes, defective materials, errors in design, drawings and specifications, poor-quality material or prefabrication errors, and incomplete design. It names five relevant clauses. The scores rate how well the contract covers each cause, not how often or how costly the cause is. Its opening line that rework sometimes costs up to 25% of contract value is cited from earlier international literature, not measured in New Zealand. It reports no New Zealand cost.
