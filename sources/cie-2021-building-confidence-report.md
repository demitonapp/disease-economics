A cost-benefit analysis of the Building Confidence Report reforms, commissioned by the national building regulator body. It rests on the CIE's own survey of residential dwelling owners in all eight states and territories: 1,606 complete responses, 2,574 defects reported, weighted to the national dwelling mix by Census. Owners estimated what each defect cost to put right. The report converts these into a cost per defect and per dwelling, and scales them with projected completions to a national cost of defects of about $2.5 billion a year. It also reviews earlier estimates (Table 2.5), none of which expresses defect cost against construction value.
