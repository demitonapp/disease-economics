A Victorian Auditor-General audit of Suburban Rail Loop East. It describes the tunnels contracts' liquidated damages regime: a daily rate for each day a separable portion is late, with larger rates after three months, capped at 10 per cent of the target outturn cost (15 per cent if the contractor chooses to raise the cap), and default termination if the cap is reached. This is a contract term on contracts not yet performed; the report gives no damages actually levied.
