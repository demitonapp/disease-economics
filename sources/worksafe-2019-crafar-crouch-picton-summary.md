A worker with no driver licence was driving a 2-axle dump truck without a seatbelt when it left the road; he died (offence March to June 2017). Charged under sections 48(1), 48(2)(c) and 36(1)(a) of the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015, a general duty. Blenheim District Court, 7 May 2019: fine $351,563; reparation $89,000 to two victims (emotional harm $62,734.66, consequential loss $3,500, after $26,000 already paid). The fine is for the whole duty failure, so none of it can be attributed to the missing licence.
