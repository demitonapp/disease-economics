A questionnaire sent to 100 practitioners (asset owners, consultants, civil contractors, surveyors, utility locators) in Auckland, Wellington, Waikato, Bay of Plenty and Northland between April and July 2020, with 34 usable responses. Respondents reported 60 utility damage incidents in 2019 tied to missing or inaccurate as-built drawings and 50 for other reasons, across 732 projects carried out by the non-asset-owner respondents. The authors say New Zealand asset owners publish no annual damage reports. From those counts it fits a line and says about 8 to 9 strikes per 100 excavations nationally, a frequency from a small sample. The paper counts incidents and gives no repair or delay cost.
