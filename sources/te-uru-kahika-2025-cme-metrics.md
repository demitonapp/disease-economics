The eighth annual survey of New Zealand's 16 regional and unitary councils on enforcement of the Resource Management Act 1991. For 2024/25 it reports 56 prosecutions concluded (82 in progress), 40 corporates and 31 individuals convicted, and $3,108,157 in fines imposed by the courts ($1,068,260 on individuals, $2,039,897 on corporates). Table 8 splits the fines by council. The figures cover every sector, with no breakdown for construction or earthworks, so they cannot be read as the cost of a site breach on a construction project.
