MBIE's impact summary for tightening the retention money regime (12 March 2020). It states that industry practice
is for retention held to be between 2 and 10 per cent of the contract price, paid after a 12 month period when the
work is confirmed defect-free, and that some contractors have used retention as working capital. The figure is a
stated practice range with no sample or method behind it. The cost-benefit estimates relate to compliance with the
regime, not to disputes.
