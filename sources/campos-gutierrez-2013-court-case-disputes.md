A text analysis of 78 published court cases from 1966 to 2012, drawn from the LexisNexis law database, covering
construction disputes in any Australian state or territory (High Court, courts of appeal, supreme courts and
administrative tribunals). Each case is coded to one or more dispute types and to five factors.

Cases coded to each factor: payments 50 of 78, damages 45, timing 38, variations 23, negligence 17. A case can
carry several factors. The cases were used to find what the dispute was about, not what it was worth or who won:
the paper records no dollar amounts. Latent or differing site conditions, retention and final accounts are not
among the five factors.
