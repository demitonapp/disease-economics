The insurance confirmation schedule for NZTA contracts under NZS 3910:2023. NZTA arranges a public and products liability policy for contractors and subcontractors on its contracts: annual policy to 1 July 2027, limit of indemnity $100,000,000, deductible $500,000 (GST inclusive), covering plant not required to be registered for road use and hired plant. Contracts above $200 million or longer than 36 months are referred separately. A contractual retention and limit, not a measure of cover that failed to reach a job.
