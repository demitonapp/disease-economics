Regulator guidance, published 30 June 2016 and confirmed current in July 2018. It says site investigation reduces
the risk of construction cost variations from unforeseen ground, and that a "lowest cost" approach to ground
investigation correlates with large cost overruns, citing a 1994 UK highways study for the evidence. It gives no
New Zealand figures for ground-condition claims or disputes.
