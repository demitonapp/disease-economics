A Te Waihanga summary of its post-construction review of the Transmission Gully motorway PPP. It names the contributors to delay and cost as external events (the Kaikoura earthquake, weather, COVID-19), budget issues, governance and consenting, an initial cost estimate set too low, and the builder not anticipating the consenting system. It gives no dollar or percentage breakdown and does not separate scope change, design change or ground conditions.
