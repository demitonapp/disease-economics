A trade-press report of a survey by insurer AMI and Ipsos of 536 homeowners (a national representative sample). Of the 170 who had done home improvement projects in the 18 months to November 2021, 54 per cent did not consider contract works insurance and a further 14 per cent did not know what it is. These are homeowners' answers about their own projects, a frequency with no dollar value. The article gives no figure for uninsured losses or for how many projects were damaged.
