The federal transport research bureau re-estimated the input mix of Australian road works in 2013 from a
survey of 8 state and territory road authorities, 233 of 558 local governments and 36 private sector
contractors. The resulting national input per cent shares (share of total cost of road works) are used to
weight its input-price index. Table B1 gives the mix for road construction and maintenance together
(labour 33.94%, materials 37.06%, equipment 29.00%); Table B2 gives the road construction sub-index:
site-based labour 24.19%, office-based labour 7.60%, bituminous materials 12.23%, cement and concrete
4.46%, quarry products 16.16%, reinforcing steel 7.92%, equipment hire and depreciation 21.69% and fuel
5.76%. The private contractors' column is 56.4% of road expenditure in the weighting.

The shares describe where road-works money goes. They are not a loss, a price movement or an overrun.
