A dispute consultancy's analysis of the three states' own published adjudication statistics,
2018-2023 (Victoria's table is headed 2018-2022). It defines a "Success Ratio" (p.26) as the amount
determined through adjudication compared to the amount claimed in adjudication applications, and
reports NSW 12%, QLD 18% and VIC 35%.

Table A: NSW $6,275,335,495 claimed and $772,580,390 determined (5,313 applications, 2,984
determinations); QLD $1,354,379,470 and $239,076,636 (2,349 and 1,370); VIC $875,076,211 and
$305,366,281 (2,122 and 1,303).

The same analysis was republished in the February 2024 eBriefing with different Victorian totals
($949,407,439 claimed, $330,423,066 determined). The December 2023 prose also gives NSW's total claimed
as $5.5 billion, against $6.28 billion in its own Table A.
