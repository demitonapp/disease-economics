The regulatory impact statement behind the 2023 retention money amendments. It states industry practice as retention of 2 to 10 per cent of contract price, paid after about 12 months, and names Ebert, Stanley Group and Tallwood as collapses that left retention unpaid. The 2019 KPMG implementation review it relies on surveyed 71 firms and found overall compliance.

Its dollar figures are a cost-benefit model: it adopts HoustonKemp's New South Wales analysis (April 2019) for insolvency and working-capital behaviour and assumes New Zealand is the same. The modelled benefit is $88.9 million a year in avoided financing costs and $7.6 million in avoided insolvency costs. These are modelled benefits of a policy, not a measured loss of retention or payment by contractors.
