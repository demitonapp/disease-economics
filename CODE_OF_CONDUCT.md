# Code of conduct

Argue with the evidence, not the person.

- Disagreement is the point of this repository. Make it with sources.
- Be civil. No harassment, personal attacks, or discrimination of any kind.
- Assume good faith, including from people whose commercial interests differ from yours, and
  declare your own when it bears on a figure you are disputing.
- Don't post private or confidential material, or anything you don't have the right to share.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and
may block people who repeatedly do. To report a problem, contact Demiton through https://demiton.io.
