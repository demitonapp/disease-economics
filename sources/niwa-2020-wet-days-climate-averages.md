Mean annual days with 1 mm or more of rain, 1991 to 2020, for stations having at least five complete years. Values include Auckland 130.1, Wellington 128.2, Hamilton 119.6, Christchurch 82.9 and Hokitika 172.4. The year given is the end of the averaging period; the page does not give a publication year.

It is a count of days, not a count of working days lost and not a cost. A 1 mm day need not stop work, and the data says nothing about extensions of time claimed or refused.
