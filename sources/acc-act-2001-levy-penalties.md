The consolidated Act as published by the Parliamentary Counsel Office. An unpaid ACC levy attracts interest at the regulated rate, and if the total is still unpaid 210 days after the due date a further 10 per cent penalty on the total unpaid amount, repeated every 180 days, with interest and penalties together capped at three times the unpaid levy (section 250). Intentionally failing to deliver a required statement is an offence (section 242). The general fine for an offence with no fine of its own is $500. These are statutory rates and caps, not observed costs, and nothing in the Act or on this page counts employers who under-declared or went uncovered.
