For employers that are uninsured or under-insured, WorkCover can recover the unpaid premium plus a penalty
equal to 100% of it, and refers some matters for prosecution. For 2025-26 to 30 September 2025 the page
reports 151 employer wage audits and AU$6,711,195 recovered, and premium raised for uninsured or under-insured
employers of AU$4,181,129. All industries, Queensland scheme only.
