The Western Australian Auditor General's report on a juvenile detention centre rebuild. Stage B core buildings were nearly 12 months late. The Department of Corrective Services paid the operator $789 000 in transitional costs for the delayed handover, and part of that was offset by $209 000 of liquidated damages applied to the Stage B building contractor. The page gives no contract value, so the damages cannot be put against the contract.
