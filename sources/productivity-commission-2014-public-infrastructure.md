A federal inquiry into the cost of public infrastructure. On contracts it explains who carries what: under
construct-only contracts the client typically carries variations and latent conditions; under design and construct,
the contractor typically carries latent conditions and design error, the client directed variations. It cites a 2007
CRC for Construction Innovation figure, second-hand, that 60 to 90 per cent of project variations come from poor
design documentation, and quotes a submission alleging a Tier 1 contractor staffs a team to generate contract
disputes. It warns that loading risk on contractors can produce costly litigation. It holds no data on dispute
amounts, outcomes or shares by cause.
