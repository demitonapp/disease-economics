Thirty-five New Zealand construction court cases found in the LexisNexis NZ database and coded by primary cause
and area of dispute; no dollar amounts are recorded. Payment issues were the area in 12 cases. Figure 3 marks
variation entitlement in 2 cases and variation procedural in 2 cases; no case is coded to delay or extension of
time, ground or site conditions, or retention and final account as an area. Employer-initiated scope changes were
the primary cause in 1 case and weather and environmental issues in 1. Claimants succeeded in 15 cases.
