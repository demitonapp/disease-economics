The Auckland follow-up to SR81, based on questionnaires from 84 architects, quantity surveyors and contractors. Contractors named non-standard sizing (53%) and non-standard design (35%) as the main causes of waste, and estimated waste by material in ranges (timber 2% to 20%, concrete 0% to 10%). The findings are perceptions of waste volume by material. No money measure is reported.
