Standardised average workers compensation premium rates as a percentage of payroll. For 2021-22 the Australian
average across all industries is 1.34% and for construction it is 2.29%. The construction table gives each
jurisdiction for 2021-22, from 1.76% (Queensland) to 4.32% (ACT private sector), with Comcare at 0.59%. These
are rates paid by insured employers; the report does not measure premium that was avoided, wages that went
undeclared or claims that fell on an uninsured employer.
