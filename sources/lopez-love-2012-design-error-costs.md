A questionnaire survey of 139 projects. Respondents' mean direct design error cost was 6.85% of contract value and their mean indirect cost 7.36%. The abstract says costs did not vary significantly by procurement method or project type, and that it reflects practitioners' perceptions. The abstract does not itself name the country; the same authors' 2014 papers report 139 Australian projects with a mean design error cost of 14.2%, which matches the two means summed (6.85 plus 7.36 is 14.21).
