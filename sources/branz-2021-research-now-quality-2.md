A three-page BRANZ summary of the NZIER study in ER49. It restates the central estimate of $995 million for defects found during new residential construction in 2018, on residential output of $18.43 billion, and the lower and upper cases of $420 million and $1,744 million. Its Table 2 prints the cost-factor row with 2.6% under the central column and 5.4% under the lower bound; that does not match ER49's Table 7 or the arithmetic ($995 million over $18.43 billion is 5.4%), so we read the figures from ER49 and use this summary only to confirm the dollar amounts.
