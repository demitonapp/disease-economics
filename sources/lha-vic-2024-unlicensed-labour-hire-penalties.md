The first construction prosecution by the Labour Hire Authority ended with penalties totalling AU$759,674 on
five companies and three directors for unlicensed labour hire across layers of subcontracting; individual
company penalties ran from AU$101,774 to AU$174,470 and each director was penalised AU$25,444. The page says
penalties can exceed AU$630,000 for a corporation. One case, one state, and a labour hire licence rather than
a contractor licence.
