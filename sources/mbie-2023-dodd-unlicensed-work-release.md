MBIE's media release on the Kaitaia District Court sentencing of Richard Barry Dodd, who carried out restricted building work on a renovation while his Licensed Building Practitioner licence was suspended and told Auckland Council he was an LBP. The court fined him $3,279.80 for carrying out restricted building work while unlicensed, ordered $4,880.81 reparation and $1,000 emotional harm to the homeowner, and fined him $1,000 for each of two false statements (one oral, one written). The release was read as republished by Scoop.
