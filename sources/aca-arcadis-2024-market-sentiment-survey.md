The following year's survey of builders and contractors (no sample size stated). It says industrial relations and rising labour costs are
raising construction costs while reducing productivity, that skilled trades, labour and consultant costs rose about
5% on average (some respondents said 15% to 20% for skilled trades), and that 71% think contracts do not allocate
risk fairly. A chart of escalation absorbed on projects during 2023 groups answers into under 10%, 10 to 25% and over
25%, with no printed values. It is a perception survey and covers construction as a whole.
