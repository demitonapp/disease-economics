WorkSafe's quarterly report to its Minister. Regulatory statistics for the quarter: 1,039 improvement notices (2,294 for 2025/26 to date), 254 prohibition notices, 12 prosecutions disposed (17 in Q1, 7 in Q4 of 2024/25). 77 per cent of workplace assessments, 81 per cent of improvement notices and 85 per cent of prohibition notices were in the four priority sectors, which include construction. These are counts of enforcement actions with no dollar value, no cause breakdown and no construction-only split. It also lists a few significant prosecutions with their fines, none of which is tied to a licence, ticket, plant or insurance failure.
