Case study of 14 projects worth $2.34 billion within an infrastructure mega-project programme, with 1,097 non-conformances analysed. The abstract gives a mean non-conformance cost of 0.41% of a project's contract value and a mean rework cost of 0.30%, names processes and methods not followed and defective materials such as concrete as the main root causes, and finds 41% of non-conformances repeated. The abstract does not name the country or the currency.
