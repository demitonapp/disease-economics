The federal review of the land transport infrastructure pipeline. It estimates $32.8 billion in known cost pressures across all projects, $14.2 billion of it on projects not yet under construction, from information supplied by the states, not independently verified by the review. It blames funding commitments made before detailed design and costing, and market volatility. It gives no breakdown by cause (ground conditions, delay, variations) and the money is the government's.
