A broker's note on the ACC levy rates for 1 April 2022 to 31 March 2025 and the experience-rating programme for employers with levies over $10,000: discounts previously up to 50 per cent and penalties up to 75 per cent, with a planned move to 100 per cent penalties. It does not address under-declared wages or uninsured contractors and is not construction-specific.
