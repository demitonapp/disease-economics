The statement of 13 May 2021 for the retention bill. It restates that retention is commonly 2% to 10% of contract value and that subcontractors risk losing it if the holder becomes insolvent. It gives MBIE's estimate of costs (about $55 million initially, $16 million a year after) and benefits ($50 to $100 million a year), all from the 2020 impact statement's model.

It is a range for what is typically held, not a measured share lost, and the benefit figures are modelled.
