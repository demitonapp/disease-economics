An authorised nominating authority's own tally of 246 published Queensland adjudication decisions,
March 2022 to August 2023. 66 (26.8%) were "no jurisdiction" decisions, where the adjudicator could not
decide the claim at all. Total payments claimed across the 246: $189,905,327.50 (including GST). Progress
payments claimed in the no-jurisdiction decisions: $70,119,333.18. Total adjudicated amounts:
$49,357,324.42.
