The 2026 edition of a New Zealand law firm's construction survey (the earlier edition is the 2020 report). It
heard from 95 professionals across New Zealand's construction sector in late 2025: engineers to the contract and
independent certifiers 30%, project managers and contract administrators 29%, principals 16%, main contractors 13%,
subcontractors 1%. Asked what will cause disputes over the next two years, respondents named delay and extension
of time claims (68%), design errors or omissions (64%), changes to the original scope of work (39%), contract
ambiguities (38%), defective or incomplete work (28%), unexpected site conditions (28%), poor project management
(25%) and payment issues (11%). On retentions, 92% say their contracts typically provide for them and, of those, 80%
use cash retentions and 20% a bond. The shares are of respondents who expect a cause, a perception rather than a
count of disputes, and the report holds no dollar amount for any dispute cause.
