Defines what the national prosecutions repository records for each defendant: the WHS Act section and
regulation section cited (free text), the main offence category (category 1, 2 or 3 for model-law
jurisdictions), whether a financial penalty was imposed and its total amount, the defendant's ANZSIC industry,
and a free-text incident description. There is no field for the kind of compliance failure (licence, plant,
insurance, accreditation, permit), so an average fine cannot be read for one cause without coding the free
text case by case.
