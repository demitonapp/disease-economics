An advocacy paper from the contractors' association. It cites a February 2022 survey of the largest contractors
in which some reported supply cost rises of up to 70% over a year, says contractors must give fixed prices for 12
to 24 months or more while suppliers cannot hold theirs, and says rise and fall clauses are uncommon outside some
construct-only forms. Table 1 is a chart of price change by item with no printed values. It argues the case for
relief and holds no measured cost of escalation to a contract.
