A conceptual causal model of construction disputes built from the literature, for the Australian industry. It
holds no new data. Its Table 1 lists, study by study, the causes other authors found. The Australian law firm
survey it cites (Blake Dawson Waldron, 2006) is listed with a ranking: variations to scope first, contract
interpretation, extension of time claims third, site conditions fourth. The ranking carries no percentages or
amounts, and this paper is a second-hand account of that survey.
