A dispute consultancy's annual report on construction disputes in North America, from a survey by
its own contract solutions team. It defines a dispute's value as "the claimed value of additional work
or event as asserted by the contractor" (p.2).

The average dispute value in 2024 was US$60.1 million (2023: US$43.0 million; 2021: US$30.1 million)
and the average length 12.5 months (2023: 14.4 months). The largest dispute was US$1.1 billion. The
report adds that 80% of claims are US$25 million or less and almost half are under US$5 million.
