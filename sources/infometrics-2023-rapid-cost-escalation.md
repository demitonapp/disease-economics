An economics consultancy's reading of Statistics New Zealand price indexes. It reports the Capital Goods Price Index up
12.5% a year in the June 2022 quarter and 6.4% in June 2023, the transport-ways index peaking at 19.1% a year at the end
of 2022, bridge construction up 39% between March 2020 and June 2023, and civil infrastructure about 26% above the trend
path it assumes. These measure how an index of prices moved economy-wide; they say nothing about how much of that fell
on a fixed-price contract or who bore it. The Statistics New Zealand figures are quoted, not read at source.
