An opinion article that cites the NZIER $2.5 billion figure, says that is nearly 10% of the sector's value lost to quality failure, and says that in Auckland one third of projects fail their final inspection (no source given). It reports a survey of 106 residential professionals on quality management. The $2.5 billion in the NZIER study is a GDP effect of removing defects, not a cost of defects, so the 10% framing is the author's, and the inspection figure is uncited.
