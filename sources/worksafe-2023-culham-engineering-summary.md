Prosecution after a timber stye supporting a 30-metre steel beam, raised by crane, collapsed and pinned a worker (22 February 2019). Section 36(1)(a) charge after a not-guilty plea. Manukau District Court, 28 November 2023: starting point $500,000, fine $400,000; reparation $50,000 emotional harm and $40,740 consequential loss shared with a co-defendant, legal costs $31,621.71. The charge concerns the temporary works and the lift, not plant registration or certification.
