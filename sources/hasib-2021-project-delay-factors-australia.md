A questionnaire sent to 50 people in the Australian construction industry, 28 of whom replied (6 clients, 11 consultants, 11 contractors), ranking 59 causes of project delay. The top causes were poor supervision and testing, interference by other parties, and skill and coordination problems. The causes are ranked by perceived importance; no cost, claim or extension-of-time outcome is measured, and ground conditions were not on the list of 59 causes.
