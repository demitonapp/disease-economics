# Governance

## Who decides

**Demiton maintains this repository and merges every change. Demiton is one person today.** Demiton's
product uses these figures, and a disease that costs more is a bigger sale, so Demiton has a
commercial interest in them. We would rather you read that here than discover it.

Because of that interest, every pull request that touches a headline figure is labelled
`headline` and its review says so.

## How fast

A first response to every dispute issue and pull request within **10 working days**.

## How a dispute is decided

1. You open a dispute issue or a pull request. Nothing changes on the figure yet.
2. Triage. If the dispute cites a source, the figure is marked `status: disputed` with a link to your
   issue. It **stays published**, flagged, until the dispute is decided. A dispute with no source is
   answered, not flagged: flagging on request would let anyone mark any figure disputed for free.
3. One of three outcomes, each with its reasoning written in the issue or pull request:
   - **Upheld** - the figure changes, and its `history` keeps the old one.
   - **Rejected** - the figure stands, and your source is recorded in the finding's `considered`
     with the reason.
   - **Unresolved** - the figure stays `disputed`.

## Appeals

**There is no appeal at present.** We would rather say so than promise a second reviewer who does not
exist. When an independent reviewer is found, they will hear appeals of rejected disputes, and this
section will name them. They will not be an author of the research this repository cites.

## Floods

If the repository is flooded with pull requests or issues that carry no evidence, we will use
GitHub's interaction limits to restrict new contributions to prior contributors for a period, and
say so here while it applies.
