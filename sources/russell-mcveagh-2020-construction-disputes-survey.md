A survey run by a New Zealand law firm of employers, main contractors, project managers, engineers to the contract and others (employers/principals 30%, main contractors 22%, sub-contractors 4%; contracts up to NZ$10m 41%, over NZ$100m 15%). The number of respondents is not stated in the sections read. It is a perception survey with no money values.

Employer variations are named as the leading cause of project delay. Respondents' anticipated causes of disputes include delays in work and extensions of time (20% of all respondents), financial issues such as slow payment and solvency (19%) and variations (13%). Unforeseen ground or site conditions are not a reported category. These are shares of respondents naming a cause, not shares of money or of claims.
