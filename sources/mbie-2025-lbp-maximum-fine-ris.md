The regulatory impact statement MBIE finalised on 5 November 2025 for Cabinet decisions on raising the maximum fine the Building Practitioners Board may impose on a Licensed Building Practitioner (LBP) from $10,000 to $20,000.

It says the Board upholds around 100 complaints or inquiries a year, that fines are the most common penalty, and that over the last four years fines have ranged from $500 to $5,000, plus costs. It gives no mean or median fine. It notes that the $10,000 maximum has not changed since 2007 and puts the maximum at 1.7 per cent of the cost to build a standalone home today. It also states MBIE's estimate that latent defects average about $23,000 per new house in 2025; it does not say who bears that cost.
