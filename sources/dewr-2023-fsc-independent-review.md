The review by Marie Boland covers the functions, powers, scope and cost recovery of the Federal Safety
Commissioner and the accreditation scheme. The PDF cover reads Final Report 2023; the department's review page
says the final report was released in June 2026. Chapter 3 quotes scheme data: after six years of
accreditation more than 60% of companies reported a premium reduction of 37%, and accredited companies have
lower average premium rates in all jurisdictions except the Northern Territory. These are scheme benefits, not
penalties or sanctions in money.
