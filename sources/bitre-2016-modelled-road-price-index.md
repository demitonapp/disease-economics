Extends the bureau's road-works input-price index back to 1910-11 and models its movements against the
Australian diesel price, average weekly earnings and the capital-expenditure-to-GDP ratio. The page states
the weights the index currently carries: site-based labour 26.7%, office-based labour 7.3%, bituminous
materials 13.7%, cement and concrete 4.1%, quarry products 13.4%, other materials (steel) 5.8%, equipment
hire and depreciation 23.2% and fuel 5.8%. These match Table B1 of Information Sheet 49 to the first decimal,
so this source corroborates the combined construction and maintenance mix, not the construction-only mix.
