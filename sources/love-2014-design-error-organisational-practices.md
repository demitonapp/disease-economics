A structured questionnaire on 139 Australian construction projects, used to find which organisational and project practices of design firms drive design error costs. The abstract gives a mean design error cost of 14.2% of original contract value, and names inadequate employee training and unrealistic design and documentation schedules set by clients as significant factors.
