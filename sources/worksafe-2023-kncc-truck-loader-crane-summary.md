Prosecution of a construction company after a stack of sheets, being lifted by a truck loader crane without being properly secured, fell and injured the operator (offence 25 May 2021). Charged under sections 36(1)(a) and 48 of the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015; the register lists four particulars, one of which is that the truck loader crane and slings had no current certificate of inspection before use. The charge is a general duty failure, so the fine cannot be split to the certificate. High Court, 19 April 2023: starting point $605,000, final fine $380,000, reduced to $240,000 for financial capacity; reparation $20,000 emotional harm and $11,882 consequential loss.
