MBIE's page on complaining about breaches of the retention money regime. A breach can draw an infringement notice
with a fine not exceeding $50,000 for an individual and $200,000 for a company. These are penalty ceilings on the
payer, not the value of retention held or disputed, and the page publishes no complaint counts or outcomes.
