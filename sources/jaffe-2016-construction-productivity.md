Firm-level productivity analysis of New Zealand construction using Statistics New Zealand's Integrated Data
Infrastructure, 2001 to 2012. It decomposes productivity growth by sub-industry into continuing firms,
reallocation between firms and entry and exit. For heavy and civil engineering it finds productivity was
raised by net entry and reallocation and lowered by a decline among continuing firms. It measures how productive firms are relative to each other, not
how many hours a job used against what it needed.
