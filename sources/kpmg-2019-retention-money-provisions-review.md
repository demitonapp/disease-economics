A review MBIE commissioned on how the retention money regime was working, dated 27 August 2019. It surveyed 223
New Zealand head contractors (71 responded, 32%) and interviewed firms, adjudicators and insolvency practitioners.
It describes retention as typically a sliding scale of up to 10% of contract value, released half at practical
completion and half at the end of the defects liability period. 27% of respondents who hold retentions had at some
point had difficulty complying with the regime. Interviewees knew of one adjudication that raised the regime and no
retention disputes before adjudicators; where retention reached adjudication the issue was its release or the
amount due. The dollar figures it gives belong to four insolvencies (about $8.35m held in retention accounts), not
to disputes nationally, and it holds no survey of the value of retentions held or disputed.
