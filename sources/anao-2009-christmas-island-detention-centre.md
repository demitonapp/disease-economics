The Auditor-General's audit of the Department of Finance and Deregulation's construction of the Christmas Island detention centre. After the main works contractor lodged seven notices of dispute in May 2007, Finance and the contractor settled by Deed in September 2007: Finance paid $10 million, released the liquidated damages it had withheld for missing Practical Completion on 19 April 2007, and waived liquidated damages up to 13 October 2007. The contractor had claimed 234.9 days of extension of time worth $46.89 million (Finance counted 89 days and $19.45 million after concurrency). The report states no dollar amount for the liquidated damages released, so it holds no liquidated-damages figure.
