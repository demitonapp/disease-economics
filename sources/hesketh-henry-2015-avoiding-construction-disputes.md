A New Zealand law firm's practitioner paper. It lists the usual causes of construction claims (inadequate
scoping, incomplete or defective design, poor contract administration, inequitable risk allocation, unrealistic
time and cost objectives) and divides disputes into payment claims (late payment, variations, delay and
disruption costs) and defect claims, which can surface long after completion. It gives no counts, amounts or
shares for any cause.
