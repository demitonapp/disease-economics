Prosecution of a vehicle inspection company after a customer waiting at its worksite was struck by a bus driven by an employee (offence about 27 February 2019). Section 36(2) charge. Hamilton District Court, 18 February 2021: fine $225,000; reparation $130,000 emotional harm, $148,885 consequential loss, $1,922.61 costs. The register records the industry as transport, not construction, and the charge is a traffic-management duty failure on a worksite, not an inspection-certificate failure.
