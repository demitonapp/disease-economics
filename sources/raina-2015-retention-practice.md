A doctoral thesis with an industry questionnaire of 187 people (59% subcontracting or specialist trade, 18% contractors). On the retention rate withheld, 58% of subcontractors said 10% and 32% said the NZS 3910 sliding scale (Table 5.12); contractors said sliding scale 53%, 10% 35%. On how often retention is lost altogether, 12% of subcontractors and 46% of contractors said never, and 44% and 43% of subcontractors said rarely and sometimes (Figure 5.8).

Table 5.16 lists subcontractors' own figures (99 replied to the section) for retention held and the share overdue; the thesis adds the overdue amounts to NZ$6,603,422, from a low-response, self-selected set. Contractors' replies (Table 5.15, 19 respondents) mostly show little overdue. The thesis gives no pooled share of contract value held or lost.
