A waste services company whose loader crane hook detached during a lift, injuring an employee (offence 5 January 2019). Charged under sections 36(1)(a), 48(1) and 2(c) of the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015, one particular being that the crane was operated without a current LT400 certificate of inspection. Tokoroa District Court, 9 February 2022: fine $15,000 over three years, costs $2,398.39. The register records the industry as waste services, not construction, and the fine is for the whole charge.
