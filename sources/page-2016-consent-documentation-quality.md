BRANZ inspectors examined 52 sets of consent drawings for new houses at several places in New Zealand, about half on site and the rest at building inspectors' offices, and scored readability and completeness. The average quality score was 3.4 out of 5. In 43% of houses not all details were provided, in 31% the uplift fixing details were missing, and 9% had no bracing details. The study counts documents with gaps; it does not price what the gaps cost on site.
