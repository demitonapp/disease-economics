A worker sentenced on 8 September 2023 in the Gladstone Magistrates Court under section 43 of the Work Health
and Safety Act 2011 (Qld) for operating a forklift without the right high risk work licence class; he had
submitted an altered licence to his employer. The page lists a penalty of AU$3,500 against a maximum fine
available of AU$20,000. One case in one state.
