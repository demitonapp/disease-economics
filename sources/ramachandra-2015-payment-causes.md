Construction Economics and Building 15(1), 43-55. An online questionnaire to 115 practitioners rated causes of payment delays and losses on a five-point importance scale and grouped them by factor analysis into contractual issues, financial strength of the players, disputes, payment process shortcomings and domino effects.

It measures how important respondents think each cause is. It reports no amount unpaid, no share of claims lost and no retention figure.
