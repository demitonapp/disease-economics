Compares unit costs of New Zealand infrastructure with similar projects in other high-income countries. It says tunnelling in difficult ground can cost up to three times as much as in favourable ground and that New Zealand's geology is likely to raise delivery costs; and that policy and institutional factors often raise cost by adding scope or design requirements. It says analysis of cost overruns is outside its scope. Its comparisons are unit costs per kilometre or lane-kilometre, not a share of contract value attributable to a cause.
