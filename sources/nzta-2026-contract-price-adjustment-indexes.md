The cost indexes the Transport Agency publishes so that road, structures and maintenance contracts can adjust their price
for cost fluctuation. They are built from Statistics New Zealand input indexes. The construction index (costs excluding
bitumen) weights labour 0.2, staff 0.15, materials 0.3 and plant and site overheads 0.35, on a December 2014 base of
1000, and prints a year-on-year escalation rate each quarter; the year to June 2022 reads 13.7%. A separate bitumen
series is set monthly in dollars a litre (December 2022: $1.1197, which is $0.4635 a litre above December 2021's $0.6562, a rise of about 71%; the file's annual column holds the difference in dollars, not a percentage). The files record how fast
prices moved for contracts that use the index, and the weights say how the index is built. They do not say how much any
contract's cost rose, or how many contracts carry the adjustment.
