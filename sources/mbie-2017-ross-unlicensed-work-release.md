MBIE's media release on a New Plymouth District Court case: Corey Ross of C.L. Renovation and Landscaping Ltd was fined $2,000 plus court costs for carrying out restricted building work without a building consent and without being licensed, after a homeowner complained. The release does not give the costs or any reparation. Read as republished by Scoop.
