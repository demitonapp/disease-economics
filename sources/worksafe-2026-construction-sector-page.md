WorkSafe's page for the construction sector. It states that construction employs 10 per cent of New Zealand workers, accounts for about 15 per cent of work-related fatalities and serious injuries over the past decade, and has 9 to 10 deaths a year. Its focus areas are vehicles and mobile machinery, work at height, dust, fumes, and communication between businesses. These are frequencies. The page gives no cost figure and no split by plant, people or licensing cause.
