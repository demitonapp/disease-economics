A consultancy's forecast of cost escalation indices for a council's capital programme. Its roading output index weights
labour 40%, plant other than structures 25%, bitumen 21% and materials other than structures 14%, on a December 2014 base
of 1000, and projects the index forward from 2023 (bitumen falling then recovering, labour pressure persisting). These
are forecasts and index weights for one council's planned work, not outturn costs on a contract.
