A law firm's note on who carries a fuel price rise under New Zealand construction contracts. Under NZS 3910 the
escalation provisions apply unless the parties opted out in the special conditions; on a fixed price contract with
fluctuations excluded the contractor is generally taken to have accepted price movement within its tendered price; and
the firm observes that since COVID-19 most contracts do not allow for cost escalation. It gives no percentages, dollar
amounts or counts of contracts.
