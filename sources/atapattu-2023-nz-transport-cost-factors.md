A questionnaire of 92 New Zealand infrastructure estimators ranking factors that affect project cost. The three most critical were frequent design changes, poor planning and scheduling, and inadequate tender documentation. These are rankings of perceived importance. The abstract gives no cost magnitude and the full text was not available.
