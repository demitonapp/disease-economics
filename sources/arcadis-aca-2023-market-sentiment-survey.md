A survey of Australian builders by Arcadis and the Australian Constructors Association. It asked how much cost escalation contractors had absorbed after every contractual mitigation (scope adjustments, variations, extensions of time). The press release says every surveyed contractor absorbed 2022 material price escalation and that for nearly half it was more than 10% of project budgets; the chart behind it prints no values and the number of respondents is not stated. Three quarters of respondents agree that contract risk allocation is handled poorly. The measure is market price escalation, not a variation, delay or ground-condition claim.
