A broad assessment of New Zealand construction productivity from macroeconomic and Statistics New Zealand
data, commissioned by the sector's productivity partnership. It finds construction productivity grew at about
half the pace of Australia's over the period it studies, and offers hypotheses for further work. It is
sector-wide, from 2013 data, and says nothing about hours against budget on civil jobs.
