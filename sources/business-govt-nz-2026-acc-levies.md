The government's guidance for employers on ACC levies. Employers are invoiced once a year (usually July) on a provisional estimate plus a prior-year adjustment; sole traders are invoiced after their tax return. Contractors who receive schedular payments still pay ACC levies. It says an employer "may face a penalty for late payment" and gives no percentage or dollar amount, no figure for employers who fail to register or under-declare wages, and nothing specific to construction.
