An audit of how the Transport Agency manages the maintenance contracts for state highways (over $500 million a year). It examines the contract model, performance measures, monitoring and the process for deciding renewals work.

It does not report on variations, claims for extra time or money, liquidated damages, ground conditions or weather, so it holds no figure for any of those causes. It is listed for the record of what a national audit of the state highway contract model does and does not measure.
