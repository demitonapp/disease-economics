A dispute consultancy's annual report. Its causes table ranks failure to properly administer the contract, poorly
drafted or unsubstantiated claims, and errors in contract documents as the top three globally, with differing
site conditions third in North America. The 2016 edition added a short section on Australia: it describes the
market (mining slowdown, road and rail pipeline in Victoria and NSW) and says typical reasons for disputes there
are haste to get product to market, inadequate risk allocation and incomplete documentation. It gives no
Australian figures and promises a fuller review in the next edition.
