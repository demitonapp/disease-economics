A survey of builders and contractors (the infographic does not state the number of
respondents). Respondents were asked how much cost escalation they had absorbed once every contractual mitigation
(scope adjustment, variation, extension of time) was applied. The release states that all respondents absorbed
some unrecoverable 2022 material price escalation, that nearly half put it above 10% of project budgets, and that
only 24% think risk is allocated fairly. The chart's bars have no printed values, and the infographic says
absorption ran 'as much as 25%'. This is people's own estimate across construction as a whole, not civil work alone.
