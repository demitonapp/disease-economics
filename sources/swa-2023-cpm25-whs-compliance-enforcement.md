Jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction tables from the regulators. For 2021-22 the Australian total is 365 legal
proceedings finalised, 301 of them ending in a conviction, order or agreement, with AU$26,050 thousand in
fines ordered by courts (publicly known fines only). The same year shows 1,546 infringement notices, 40,465
improvement notices and 5,700 prohibition notices. Victoria, New South Wales and the ACT report only
successful prosecutions. Nothing is broken down by kind of breach or by industry.
