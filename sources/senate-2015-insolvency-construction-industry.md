The committee's chapter on what construction insolvencies cost, built on ASIC's initial external administrators' reports. For 2013-14 it counts 2,153 insolvent construction companies and estimates, by taking the lowest dollar figure in each deficiency band, a shortfall of liabilities over assets of at least $1.625 billion (Table 3.1; the CFMEU's median-based estimate is $2.70 billion). Unsecured creditors, the class that includes unpaid subcontractors, were paid nothing in 1,974 of the 2,153 cases (91.7%, Table 3.4). The chapter also totals unpaid employee entitlements ($56.9 to $136.6 million in 2013-14) and unpaid tax ($487 million). It states no figure for retention held or lost.
