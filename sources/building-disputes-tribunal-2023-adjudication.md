Twenty years of statutory adjudications under New Zealand's Construction Contracts Act 2002
administered by the Building Disputes Tribunal, one of the Authorised Nominating Authorities, from its
registry records and per-case surveys.

Averaged across all claims, as a percentage of the value of the claim: claimant's own costs 19.2%,
respondent's costs 17.64%, adjudicator's fee 16.37%. The shares fall steeply with claim size: under
$30,000, 21.6% / 49.59% / 33.73%; $1-5 million, 11.99% / 8.29% / 4.15%; over $10 million, 1.74% /
0.77% / 0.40%.
