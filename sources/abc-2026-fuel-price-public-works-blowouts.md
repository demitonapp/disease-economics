A news report in which the Civil Contractors Federation chief executive says diesel is about 79% of civil
construction's energy use and about 7.75% of any tender, which he puts at an extra $46 million on a billion-dollar
project, and the Master Builders Australia chief executive says fixed-price arrangements are forcing most builders
to absorb fuel surcharges. Both are statements to a reporter by industry lobbyists; the article does not show
how the $46 million was worked out or which fuel price change it assumes.
