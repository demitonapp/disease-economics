The 2014 update of the bureau's road-works input-price index. It reports a rise of 0.49 index points between
2012-13 and 2013-14, led by site-based labour and diesel fuel, against falls in equipment hire and
depreciation, steel and bituminous materials, and compares it with the ABS output price index for road and
bridge construction, which rose 2.5 index points over the same year. It uses the 2013 input per cent shares.
It reports price movement in index points, not a cost.
