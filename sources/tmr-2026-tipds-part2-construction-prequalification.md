Queensland's construction prequalification manual, which refers to the national prequalification terms and to
mutual recognition arrangements. The department may suspend, downgrade or cancel a contractor's
prequalification after a show cause notice. Cancellation examples include unsatisfactory performance,
administration or creditor arrangements, and an adverse change in management systems or key personnel. A scan
found no dollar amount attached to any sanction.
