A practitioners' society's guidance for contract administrators on how fuel and freight price rises and supply delays
play out under the NZS 3910 family of contracts. It sets out an order of steps (read the contract baseline, check
fluctuation settings, check time and cost recovery, keep records) and suggests agreeing baseline indices such as the
diesel price, container shipping rates and the NZTA bitumen cost adjustment series. It contains no cost figures and no
count of contracts.
