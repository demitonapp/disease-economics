MBIE's monthly counts of claims under the Weathertight Homes Resolution Services Act 2006, read from the Internet
Archive's copy of the file because the MBIE address refuses scripted downloads. As at 31 January 2019 MBIE had
received 7,377 claims for 12,810 properties and completed 8,745 property assessments; the sheets split claims by
status and open claims by the council that issued the building consent. They hold counts only: no claim value, no
repair cost and no award. The claims are homeowners' weathertightness defect claims, not disputes at the close of a
contract between contractor and principal.
