A plant hire company delivered a skid steer loader to a construction site without ensuring its safety devices worked (offence 26 May 2015); the operator's leg was crushed and amputated above the knee. Charged under sections 18(1)(b) and 50(1)(a) of the Health and Safety in Employment Act 1992. Auckland District Court, 5 April 2016: no fine because the company was in liquidation; reparation $66,515.20 shared 60 per cent by Adams Machinery and 40 per cent by the site operator. One old case, decided under the earlier Act, about plant condition and not registration.
