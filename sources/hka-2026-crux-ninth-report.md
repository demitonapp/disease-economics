A consultancy's analysis of 2,410 projects in 122 countries it investigated, worth US$2.56tn. The release says scope
change was blamed for disputes on 33.4% of projects and design failures on 40.5%; average extension-of-time claims
fell to 48.8% of contract schedules on projects completing from 2020, with contested costs at 28.4% of budget. By
region, unforeseen physical conditions triggered claims on 17.4% of projects in the Americas. Oceania is reported
only for claimed costs (13.3% of budgets on later projects) and schedule overrun (60.0%), with no split by cause.
The release carries no Australian national figure.
