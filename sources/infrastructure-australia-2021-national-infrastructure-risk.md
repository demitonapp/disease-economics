A national risk assessment built from a market sounding survey of government and private executives, subject
experts and consultations. Section 4.9 calls unpredictable ground conditions a common, high-impact risk on
transport and tunnelling projects, says tenderers often receive geotechnical reports on a no-reliance basis, and
charts how government and private respondents think latent condition risk should be shared. It also notes insurers
reacting to a growth in claims for delay. The material is perceptions of risk, with no dispute counts, amounts or
outcomes.
