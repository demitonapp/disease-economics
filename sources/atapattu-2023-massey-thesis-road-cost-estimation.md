A doctoral thesis, read in full text, that first measures how far New Zealand road projects overran and then builds an
estimating model. Chapter 4 takes 106 completed road projects whose records were obtained from the New Zealand
Transport Agency and Auckland Transport (the other completed projects of the past twenty years lacked usable records),
and compares each project's final account with its "forecasted cost". The table reports a mean percentage cost overrun
of 19.5% over the 106 projects, a median of 18%, and 96% of projects over. The forecast stage is not named in the table
or its text, and the dollar rows of the same table do not reconcile with its percentages (the small-project sum of
overruns is larger than the small-project sum of total cost). Chapter 3 also reports a survey of 92 estimators who ranked
53 causes of overrun by a significance index; those are perceptions, with no dollar share by cause.
