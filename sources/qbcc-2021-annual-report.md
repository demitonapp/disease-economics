The Adjudication Registry section reports every valid adjudication application under the Building
Industry Fairness (Security of Payment) Act 2017 for 2020-21. For the 187 decisions released in
the year, the total value of claims was $82,254,751.00 and the total adjudicated amount $37,503,111.00.
