A pilot questionnaire of 38 architects, quantity surveyors and contractors, mostly in the Wellington region, on how design and procurement drive material waste on building sites. Contractors' own estimates of waste by material span wide ranges (for example plasterboard 5% to 15%, concrete 0% to 15%). It reports opinions on what reduces waste (standard sizing, avoiding variations on site, contracts that reward waste reduction) and no cost.
