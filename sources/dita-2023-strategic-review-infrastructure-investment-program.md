The federal review of the land transport program. It finds an estimated $32.8 billion of known cost pressures on
all projects in the program, $14.2 billion of them on projects not yet under construction, and says escalation,
delays and scope changes are handled ad hoc. The summary does not split the $32.8 billion by cause, and it is the
federal owner's budget pressure, not a contractor loss.
