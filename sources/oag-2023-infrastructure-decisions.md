The Auditor-General's audit of how the New Zealand Upgrade Programme (NZUP, announced in January 2020) and the
Shovel-Ready Programme were decided. For the transport part of NZUP, a $6.8 billion allocation, a mid-2020
Oversight Group and a "re-baselining" exercise found that 21 of the 26 announced transport projects could
not be delivered within the funding allocated, and the original programme could not be delivered for $6.8
billion (5.32). On 31 May 2021 Cabinet agreed to additional Crown funding of $1.926 billion to take forward a
revised package, funding agreed projects at their new cost estimates and providing contingency (5.12, 5.33);
some projects were rescoped (5.33).

The audit's finding is about the quality of information at announcement ("confidence in the baseline
information is much lower than would be expected", 5.30). It does not state an outturn cost.
