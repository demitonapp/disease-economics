A November 2019 RMIT study for SafeWork NSW on the causes of crane incidents in construction and how to
prevent them. It names worker skill and competency, licensing systems and operator competence among the
factors and strategies. It draws on SafeWork NSW data. A keyword scan of the whole text found no cost per
incident or fine amount. New South Wales only.
