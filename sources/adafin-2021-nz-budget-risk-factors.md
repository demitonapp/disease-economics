Journal of Engineering, Design and Technology 19(1), 41-61; read as the accepted manuscript. An online survey of 64 New Zealand project managers ranked risk factors behind the gap between design-phase elemental cost plans and the out-turn tender sum. The top five were changes in owner or stakeholder requirements, experience of the project team, site condition information, competency of consultants, and information flow and quality. The abstract reports a 23.86% drift in budgeted costs.

The gap is between a cost plan and the tender price, before award. It is not the value of variations, latent conditions or delay costs claimed or missed on a contract that is under way.
