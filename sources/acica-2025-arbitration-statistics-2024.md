The national arbitration centre's caseload for 2024: 54 cases administered, 25 new; construction was 30.8% of the new
administered arbitrations, by sector. Eight of the 13 new arbitrations were valued, at over AUD 74.45 million in
total. It reports no split of construction disputes by cause and no dollar figures by sector.
