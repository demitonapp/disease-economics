The Adjudication Registry section reports every valid adjudication application under the Building
Industry Fairness (Security of Payment) Act 2017 for 2022-23. For the 172 decisions released in
the year, the total value of claims was $152,408,699.17 and the total adjudicated amount $21,858,656.22.
