National workers compensation statistics. For construction the table shows 16,600 serious claims (12.0% of
all), a claim incidence rate of 17.2, a frequency rate of 8.8, a median time lost of 8.5 weeks and a median
compensation paid of AU$18,500. A footnote says that for the preliminary year the median compensation column
uses the last non-preliminary period (2021-22). The table is by industry only; it does not split claims by
whether a licence, ticket, registration or insurance cover was missing.
