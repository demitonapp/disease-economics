Every security-of-payment adjudication application lodged in NSW in 2011-12, as reported by the
Authorised Nominating Authorities: 1,112 lodged, 779 with a determination released.

On the 779 determinations, $153,143,608 was claimed and $77,903,361 adjudicated: in the report's words,
adjudicators "awarded claimants about half of the aggregate amount claimed". Fig. 4 scopes both totals
to applications where a determination was released. By claim size: claims under $100,000 (74% of
determinations) were awarded about 84% of the amount claimed; $100,000 to $500,000, about 63%.
