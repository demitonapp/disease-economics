Australasian Journal of Construction Economics and Building 11(2), 22-33. Two record sets: liquidators' reports for 65 construction companies whose liquidations finished 2007 to 2010 (general construction 27, property developers 22, trade services 16), and 40 payment disputes in the High Court from 2008 to 2010.

In liquidation, every general construction company in the sample paid its trade creditors nothing; 17 of 22 property developers paid nothing and one paid 11.89 cents in the dollar; 12 of 16 trade service companies paid nothing, about 20% paid pro rata and one paid in full. Of the 40 court cases, 16 (40%) ended with the claimant paid in full but late, 4 (10%) paid in part, 10 (25%) a loss and 8 (20%) likely loss or delay (Table 3); in 21 of 40 the claimant used Construction Contracts Act provisions. The authors classify outcomes themselves and say the data cannot tell which tier of the contracting chain bore the loss.
