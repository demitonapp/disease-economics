A news report relaying an MBIE statement on two cases. Terry Draper (Taupo) was ordered to pay $3,000 plus costs for claiming to be an LBP and doing restricted work; the article does not label that amount a fine. George Faulkner (Hutt City District Court) was fined $2,000 for carrying out restricted building work unlicensed, with $4,500 reparation and costs. A press account, not the court record.
