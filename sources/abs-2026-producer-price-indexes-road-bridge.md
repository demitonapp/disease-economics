The ABS output price index for road and bridge construction, Australia. It stood at 146.6 in September 2025,
148.2 in March 2026, 154.9 in June 2026 and 155.4 in September 2026. The change over the year to September
2026 is therefore 6.0%, and the June 2026 quarter alone rose 4.5% (our arithmetic from the published index
numbers). It is a price charged to clients, not a cost to the contractor and not a share of a contract.
