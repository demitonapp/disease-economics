A quantity surveyor's re-pricing of three "typical" Wellington three-waters renewal contracts, first priced in August
2020 and updated in August 2021, after suppliers were re-contacted for the fourth quarter of 2021. Diesel is reported up
about 13% and bitumen products 8-10% in the Wellington region in the quarter, plant hire rates 3-5% (operated plant
5-7%), and the three specimen contracts together up 5-6% since September, with a note that they contain little of the
volatile materials. It is one consultant's estimate for one region and one quarter, and it warns that long fixed-price
contracts face cash-flow pressure without saying how many do.
