A one-page summary of the ABS output price index for road and bridge construction. The national index rose
0.4% in the September quarter 2019 and 1.7% over the year, against a ten-year average of 0.6% a quarter
and 2.4% a year. It reports what road and bridge builders charge, not what their inputs cost them.
