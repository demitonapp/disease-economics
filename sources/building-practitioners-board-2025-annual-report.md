The Board's report on its 2024/25 year. Of 81 penalties imposed in disciplinary decisions, 59 were fines, 11 censures, 7 licence cancellations, 2 suspensions and 2 training orders; 77 per cent of sanctioned LBPs received a fine. A decision can carry more than one penalty. The report counts penalties and does not state the dollar amount of any fine. The Chair notes that the Board cannot investigate an LBP whose licence is suspended, that many complaints involve inadequate supervision, and that the Ministry's only recourse for unlicensed work is the courts.
