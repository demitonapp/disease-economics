A consultancy briefing estimating that stagnant construction productivity costs Australia $62 billion a year in
lost work, measured against a counterfactual of productivity keeping pace with other industries. It is an
economy-wide modelled opportunity cost across all construction, with no share of any project's cost, and the full
report was not read.
