The Board's report on its 2023/24 year. Fines were again the most common penalty (56 fines, 8 censures, 7 licence cancellations, 2 suspensions, 1 training order). The report states the count of each penalty, not the dollar amount of any fine, so it gives no cost per disciplinary breach.
