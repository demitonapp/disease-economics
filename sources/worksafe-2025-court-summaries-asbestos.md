WorkSafe's public register of court summaries (608 entries when read), each giving the defendant, industry, facts, charged sections, plea, decision, fine and reparation. A search for "asbestos" returned 24 entries; 7 were decided under the Health and Safety in Employment Act 1992, 2 were withdrawn and one was an appeal of an entry already counted.

Fines in entries decided under the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015 for asbestos offences in building and construction, as the register states them: Topham Holding Limited $10,000 (2017, asbestos regulations 21(3)(b)); Richard John Knight $3,000 (2018, no Class A or B removal licence); John Carstairs Robertson $35,000 (2018, removal without notifying WorkSafe or containment); Crafar Crouch Construction Limited $318,750 (2018, demolishing two asbestos-contaminated buildings, three charges); Glenn Warwick Sibley $15,000 (2020, removal of ceiling tiles without risk assessment or licence); Ecologically Sound Demolition Limited $16,000 (2020, removal without the licensed removalist present and without notifying WorkSafe); Lawrence Gannaway $1,100 (2023, regulation 26); Inspired Enterprises Limited $23,375 after appeal (2023, no asbestos survey; the District Court fine of $52,500 was reduced on appeal); Wilson Building Timaru Limited $66,000 (2023, a worker not licensed to remove asbestos-backed vinyl released fibres over four days).

Entries left out: Dong SH Auckland Limited ($255,000) and Quick Earth Moving Limited ($150,000), where a wall collapse was the main charge and asbestos was found afterwards; Essential Homes Limited ($30,000), charged mainly with breaching a prohibition notice; and entries whose industry the register records as hospitality or retail.
