The companion dataset report to Te Waihanga's benchmarking. Its section 5 gives international reference classes for cost and schedule overruns by project type (for example about seven in ten rail projects overrun, nearly 30% by more than 50%), and states that estimated-versus-actual data were lacking for New Zealand projects, so it could not test whether New Zealand overruns differ. It does not attribute overruns to scope change or any other cause.
