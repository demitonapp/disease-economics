Safe Work Australia refreshed its national prosecutions dashboard with 2024 data and listed the key findings.
317 prosecutions were recorded for 2024, 47% of them in construction; 97% ended in a financial penalty; the
average financial penalty per case was AU$116,979, against AU$138,724 in 2023. The dataset page says the
repository holds criminal prosecutions under WHS laws since 1 January 2020, compiled from the regulators'
public reports, one record per defendant. The dashboard is a Power BI embed and was later refreshed with 2025
data; that refresh was not read.
