A contractors' association report arguing for national reform. It charts the ABS multifactor productivity measure
for construction against other industries and quotes an Oxford Economics estimate that lifting construction
productivity to the economy-wide average would unlock an extra $56 billion in construction capacity a year. Its
measures are productivity indexes and an economy-wide capacity estimate, not a cost on a contract.
