The Adjudication Registry section reports every valid adjudication application under the Building
Industry Fairness (Security of Payment) Act 2017 for 2021-22. For the 176 decisions released in
the year, the total value of claims was $51,816,750.76 and the total adjudicated amount $15,854,533.66.
