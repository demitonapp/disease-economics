A data sheet from the Federal Safety Commissioner's reporting. Of 1,737 high-risk hazard incidents reported by
accredited companies in 2015-2019, mobile plant was the most frequent with 548, then falls from height (372),
road or traffic (249), electrical (144) and tunnelling (125). Of 4,447 corrective action reports against high-
risk hazard criteria in 2017-2019, mobile plant drew 1,954 (44%) and scaffolding 590 (13%). The most common
mobile plant finding concerned safe systems of work (15.3% of mobile plant reports), then inspection programs
(13.5%). Counts only; no cost.
