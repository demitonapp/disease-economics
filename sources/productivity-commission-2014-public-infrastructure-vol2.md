The Commission's national inquiry into public infrastructure costs and delays. Volume 2 mentions liquidated damages only in passing: as one form of penalty in incentive contracting, as a risk head contractors face for industrial delay (quoting a commentator on one desalination plant: $15 million for one day late), and as a provision of up to 10 per cent in the design-and-construct contract of one financed project. It gives no measure of damages actually levied or of extension-of-time claims refused.
