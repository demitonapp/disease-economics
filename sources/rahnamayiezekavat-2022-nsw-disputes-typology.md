An analysis of 230 New South Wales court cases from 2002 to 2021 taken from LexisNexis, coded by main category
of dispute. Table 3 gives each category's share of all cases: payment and reimbursement 31%, quality 13%, time 6%,
contract 7%, variation 3%, scope 2%, finance and cost 2%, debt 1%. The paper counts cases; it reports no dollar
amounts. New South Wales only.
