A calculator for contractors to estimate a fuel surcharge on a contract. It applies a default 7.75% of contract
value as 'fuel-impacted' (5% direct fuel plus 2.75% indirect fuel in materials and subcontracts) to the
change in terminal gate price since award. The page says the percentages are an example based on feedback from
projects the federation was given, and that individual projects differ. It shows a typical civil cost structure
(subcontractors 34%, plant and equipment 24%, materials 14%, labour 9%, direct fuel 5%, overhead 10%, margin 4%).
These are guideline proportions, not measured outcomes.
