ACC's quarterly statistics deck for work-related injury claims, July 2026. Table 1 gives all-industry totals, including active claim costs of $1,254,610,727 across 271,126 active claims in 2025 (accredited employer claims excluded). Table 2 gives new claims by industry and year: construction had 32,370 in 2023, 28,864 in 2024 and 27,121 in 2025. Costs are not split by industry, so the deck gives no cost per construction claim, and it says nothing about insurance, licensing or compliance.
