A questionnaire of 92 New Zealand infrastructure estimating practitioners ranked 53 literature-derived causes of
transport project cost overrun in ten categories, and found ten significant. The abstract finds that cost overrun
is primarily affected by pre-contract-stage causes; the leading factors it names are frequent design changes, poor
planning and scheduling and inadequate tender documentation. It reports the opinions of estimators, not measured
project costs, and the abstract gives no overrun percentage or share by cause.
