A painter pleaded guilty on 19 March 2021 in the Brisbane Magistrates Court to three offences after workers
used a high pressure hose on an asbestos roof and he did not comply with an improvement notice. The page lists
a penalty of AU$3,000 against a maximum fine available of AU$153,600, plus AU$1,000 in professional and legal
costs. One case in one state.
