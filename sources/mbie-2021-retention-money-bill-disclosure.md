The disclosure statement for the retention bill (13 May 2021). It says retention money is commonly between 2% and
10% of the contract value and often paid out after 12 months, following the defects liability period, and that
subcontractors are at risk of losing retention held for them if the contractor becomes insolvent. It gives no
count or value of retention withheld or disputed.
