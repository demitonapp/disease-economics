A quantity surveyor's check, for the NSW pricing regulator, of the road, drainage and earthworks quantities
in one council's developer contributions plan. It found a significant difference in earthworks quantities
between its quantities and the council's, and concluded that the design and construct cost of the road
upgrade works should fall by about $5.2 million, 6% of the council's estimate. It compares two estimates
of quantities before any work was built. It does not measure growth during construction.
