The statutory advisor's annual review of whether the market can deliver the national infrastructure pipeline.
On p.7 it says labour is roughly two-thirds of costs in land-transport infrastructure construction (no source
given for that proportion), that industry sentiment puts the price escalation of non-labour inputs over the
last twelve months at about 10-20%, and that almost 47 cents of every dollar a construction company spends
goes to outsourced services such as labour hire, design consultants and equipment rental (ABS 'intermediate
services inputs'). On p.60 the same measure is given as 46.5% of all input costs for the industry, up from
about 40 cents in 1995-96. Construction multifactor productivity growth is reported as -0.8 (p.60).
