The sibling of the 2016 Grattan overruns report, covering megaprojects. On input prices it is descriptive: it shows
the ABS road and bridge construction producer price index flat between late 2013 and late 2016 and rising
strongly after, and links the run of underruns around 2015 to a falling market. It gives no figure for how much of
an overrun came from input price rises on a fixed price.
