A regulatory impact statement on improving adjudication under the Construction Contracts Act 2002. It cites a 2010
Research New Zealand survey in which 19% of residential consumers reported a major dispute with their building
practitioner, and says Authorised Nominating Authorities had heard about 700 adjudication claims since the Act began.
It does not split disputes by cause or give values.
