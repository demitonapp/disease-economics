International Journal of Construction Supply Chain Management 4(2), 22-33. A questionnaire survey of consultants and contractors, mostly Wellington-based: 118 responses, 36 usable. Respondents rated 21 contractual risk factors in six categories on a five-point significance scale: site conditions first, then main contractor, pricing, subcontractor, external and client-related risks. In Table 1 unforeseen site conditions score a mean 3.50 and inclement weather 2.89. The paper says there is little or no New Zealand information on these risks, which leads contractors to over- or under-compensate for them.

The ratings are people's own judgement of how significant a risk is. The paper gives no amount of money and no variation, delay cost or liquidated damages measure.
