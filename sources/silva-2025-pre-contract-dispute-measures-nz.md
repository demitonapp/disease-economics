Interviews with fourteen New Zealand industry experts, coded in NVivo, to identify pre-contract steps that avoid
disputes (variation pricing rates and timeframes, extension-of-time mechanisms agreed in advance, pre-contract site
investigations, clear scope). Counts are numbers of respondents who raised a measure. The paper reports no dispute
frequencies or dollar amounts.
