A survey of 52 Belgian public infrastructure stakeholders (public tendering authorities, general
contractors, design offices and others; 34% response rate), alongside a literature review.
Respondents were asked to assign 100% of the additional cost caused by overruns across the parties
(Section 2.2, p.7).

On average they assigned 49% to public tendering authorities, 40.5% to contractors, 7.7% to engineering
and design offices and 2.8% to others (p.18). Contractors assigned themselves 50.7%; the supporting
services group assigned contractors 35.6%.
