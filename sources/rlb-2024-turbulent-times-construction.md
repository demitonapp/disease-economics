A cost consultancy's market commentary. Its own research on construction output per worker says that since 2013 the
workforce grew 26.5%, hours per worker fell 2.0% and output per worker fell 25.4%. It repeats that the Productivity
Commission's 2024 bulletin put construction labour productivity down 1.8% in 2022-23. These are productivity rates
for all construction, not costs.
