Prosecution of a construction company after a stack of sheets, being lifted by a truck loader crane without being properly secured, fell and injured the operator (offence 25 May 2021). Charged under section 36(1)(a) of the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015 for a general duty failure, not for registration, inspection or certification of the crane. High Court, 19 April 2023: starting point $605,000, final fine $380,000, reduced to $240,000 for financial capacity; reparation $20,000 emotional harm and $11,882 consequential loss.
