The Adjudication Registry section reports every valid adjudication application under the Building
Industry Fairness (Security of Payment) Act 2017 for 2023-24. For the 234 decisions released in
the year, the total value of claims was $225,306,008.71 and the total adjudicated amount $61,524,294.13.
