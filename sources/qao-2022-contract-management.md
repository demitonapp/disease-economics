A Queensland Audit Office performance audit of how two state departments managed contracts for new
infrastructure (schools and other public buildings), tabled 17 May 2022. It defines a contract variation as
"the process of adding to or altering the terms of an original contract in a way that is mutually agreed by
both parties to the contract".

The contracts reviewed "had over $127 million in contract variations, equating to 9 per cent of the cost of
the projects we examined". On one project, Fortitude Valley State Secondary College, variations totalled
$14.5 million, 21.5% of the original contract value.
