A Victorian Auditor-General audit of regional rail upgrades. On the Murray Basin Rail Project, the May 2018 settlement agreement valued the contractor's completed works at $204 million, released the contractor from most defect obligations and from any exposure to liquidated damages for late completion, and set V/Line's final settlement sum at 10.5 per cent of the original contract price. It does not say what liquidated damages had accrued, so it shows damages forgone but not their size.
