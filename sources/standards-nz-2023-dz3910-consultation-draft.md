The draft marked "do not use as a standard", for the revision of the contract form used on most New Zealand
building and civil work. It sets the mechanisms disputes turn on: the contractor notifies physical conditions that
an experienced contractor could not reasonably have foreseen at tender and they are treated as a variation (9.5);
extension-of-time grounds and notice (10.3); a practical completion certificate; a final account served within a
month of the final completion certificate; and retention against defects. It is contract wording, with no dispute
data.
