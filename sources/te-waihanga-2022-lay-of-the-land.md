Compares unit costs of eight types of New Zealand infrastructure with other high-income countries and
explains the differences by input costs, productivity, physical context and policy. It states that analysis
of infrastructure project cost overruns is out of its scope. Its breakdown of project cost by labour,
materials, equipment and land (Figure 6) is Australian data (Productivity Commission 2014, BITRE 2018), not
New Zealand data, so no New Zealand figure is taken from it. Its international comparison finds New
Zealand construction equipment costs about 50% above the median high-income country and wages about 35%
below Australia's.
