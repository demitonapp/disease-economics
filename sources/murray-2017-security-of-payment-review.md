A national review of the state and territory security of payment laws, with a survey of 526 construction contractors commissioned for it (46.8% Queensland, 30.8% Western Australia, 17.9% New South Wales; 73% of respondents in firms of 20 or fewer people). It reports how often invoices are paid late, how many contractors have used the statutory claim process, and the average dollar amount of the most recent claim ($221 633). It also reproduces the Australian Small Business and Family Enterprise Ombudsman's construction results (21% of construction businesses owed more than $100 000 in late payments) and ASIC counts of construction insolvencies (about 1,743 a year, 22% of all). Retention is discussed as policy, with no measure of how much is held or lost.
