Benchmarks of material sent to landfill, recycling or waste-to-energy from Australian commercial and residential sites, reported per square metre. It estimates that wasted material averages 141 kg per square metre, 22% of the material weight, and prices this at up to about $384 per square metre by assuming wasted material costs the same as used material. The methodology note says the industry practice is to over-order by about 10% to guard against delays, and does not give a source or a measured rate for it. The dollar values are the report's own extrapolation, not recorded costs.
