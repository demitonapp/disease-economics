International Journal of Construction Supply Chain Management 4(2), 22-33. A questionnaire survey of consultants and contractors ranked 21 contractual risk factors in six categories by significance: site conditions first, then main contractor, pricing, subcontractor, external and client-related risks. The abstract says there is little or no New Zealand information on these risks, which leads contractors to over- or under-compensate for them.

Only the abstract was read. It gives a ranking of risk categories, not any amount of money, and names no variation, delay cost or liquidated damages measure.
