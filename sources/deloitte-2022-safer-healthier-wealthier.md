A general equilibrium model of the Australian economy without work-related injury and illness. It estimates
the economy would be AU$28.6 billion larger each year (about 1.6% higher GDP) and wages 1.3% higher. These are
modelled economy-wide gains, not costs borne by an employer for a missed licence, ticket, registration or
insurance.
