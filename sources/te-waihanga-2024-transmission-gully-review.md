An independent review (led by Steve Richards, commissioned through Te Waihanga) of one New Zealand road, the
Wellington Transmission Gully public-private partnership. It reports opening about two years late, $400.3
million of settlement payments up to the March 2022 opening, and a $186.5 million settlement of the
consenting-related enabling works claim. It finds the Affordability Threshold was set too low and that the
winning bid fitted under it.

The review gives no single original-versus-final cost ratio in the executive summary. It is one project and
the matters are partly in litigation.
