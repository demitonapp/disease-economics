Annual data on the Australian Government Work Health and Safety Accreditation Scheme; accredited companies are
eligible to be contracted for Australian Government funded projects. 606 companies held accreditation at the
end of 2024 and 48 new accreditations were approved. The office ran 657 audits and raised 3,319 corrective
action reports (a stated compliance rate of 80.9%; 612 major, 2,707 minor). The highest issue rate for
accredited companies was 52% for the subcriterion on temporary structures being installed by a competent
person (40 reports in 77 tests). Table 23 reports the change in accredited companies' workers compensation
premium rates over time. No money penalty and no count of suspensions or cancellations is given.
