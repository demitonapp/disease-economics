A doctoral thesis on cost overruns in New Zealand road projects. A questionnaire (92 completed responses from New Zealand professionals) rated 53 cost factors from the literature on a five-point scale; cost data came from 106 road projects completed 2002 to 2022, supplied by Auckland Transport and the New Zealand Transport Agency (the author says more projects exist but their records were not usable). Overrun is the final account minus the forecast cost, as a share of the forecast. The mean across the 106 projects was 19.5% with a standard deviation of 16.07% (Table 4.1; the abstract rounds it to 20%). It is not split by cause. The dollar rows of Table 4.1 (sum of overruns NZ$815.7 million, of total cost NZ$5,077 million) do not add up consistently by size band, so only the percentage is used.

In Table 3.2 'unforeseen ground conditions' has a severity index of 78% (sixth of the 53 factors), 'unpredictable weather conditions' 62% (27th) and 'variations and extra works' 64% (21st). These are respondents' ratings of how much a factor matters. The thesis reports no value of unclaimed variations, latent conditions, delay costs or liquidated damages, and the project data has no claim ledger.
