The regulations that set certificates of inspection and competence for cranes, pressure equipment and passenger ropeways. Part 6 and regulation 39, which carried the offences and penalties, were revoked on 4 April 2016 by section 232 of the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015. A missing certificate of inspection is therefore no longer a stand-alone offence with its own penalty; it is pleaded as one particular of a general duty charge under that Act. The regulations hold no dollar figure.
