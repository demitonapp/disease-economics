A regulatory impact statement on improving adjudication under the Construction Contracts Act, built on a literature review, two consultations and market research on residential construction disputes. It reports that 19% of residential consumers felt they had a major dispute with their builder, and gives the fees of the Disputes Tribunal, mediation and courts.

It is about residential consumer disputes and the cost of resolving them. It has no figure for unpaid payment claims, retention or subcontractor losses.
