A state chapter's media release. Its chief executive says fuel can be up to 30% of project input costs, diesel is
up 50% in a few weeks, and the cost to seal a regional road has risen 30%, about $9,000 a kilometre, on current
bitumen prices alone. No method, sample or source is given for any of these, and it covers Tasmania only.
