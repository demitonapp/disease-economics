The Commonwealth Auditor-General's audit of how Defence managed the redevelopment of its propellant plant at Mulwala, New South Wales, and the five deeds it negotiated with the prime contractor, Lend Lease, between 2011 and 2015. Paragraph 3.1 gives the June 2007 fixed-price design-and-construct contract as valued at $263 million. Box 2 and its Note b record the liquidated damages actually applied: $1.3 million for late Practical Completion (May to December 2010) and $5.2 million for late Final Acceptance (December 2014 to April 2015), the latter deducted from a payment due at Final Acceptance. The same box shows Defence keeping $1.3 million of $1.8 million accrued in the first period. Table 3.1 lists liquidated damages paid by Lend Lease as $7 million, which does not match the $6.5 million in Note b. One project, so it is a case, not a sample.
