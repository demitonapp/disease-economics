The conference version of Grattan's study of cost overruns on Australian transport projects of $20 million or more, 2001 to 2015. It measures overruns from the first announced cost and relates them to appraisal and political factors (election-commitment projects overran 23% more on average; projects contracted during or after the financial crisis were 21% less likely to overrun). It does not split overruns by cause, so it says nothing on latent conditions, delay costs or variations as such, and its money is the owner's.
