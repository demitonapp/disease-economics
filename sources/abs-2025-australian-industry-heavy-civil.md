The ABS annual industry accounts. For heavy and civil engineering construction in 2024-25 the cube gives, in
$ millions: total income 132,471; total expenses 123,216; total labour costs 26,308 (wages and salaries 22,577
plus employer superannuation 2,179 and other labour costs); purchases of goods and materials, including
capitalised purchases, 37,773; operating profit before tax 8,631; industry value added 36,847. Employment at
the end of June 2025 was 165,900. Labour costs here are those of the industry's own employees, so work
bought from subcontractors sits in other expenses.
