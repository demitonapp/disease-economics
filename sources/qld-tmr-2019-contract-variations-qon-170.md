The Minister's answer lists the net value of variations to Transport and Main Roads contracts above $2 million: $753,685,537 on $8,873,345,229 of awarded value over 2015-16 to 2018-19 as at 11 March 2019. It names latent conditions, weather and market shifts among the reasons variations arise, but gives no split by reason. Net variations include decreases, so they are not a measure of work done and unpaid.
