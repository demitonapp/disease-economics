28 completed Australian infrastructure mega projects (over A$500m, road, rail, water and social
infrastructure, PPP and design-and-construct), commenced 2000 to 2015, delivered by Tier 1
contractors, with profit figures from the contractors' own open-book project data.

Across the 28, the tendered profit was A$3,328m (9% of contract value) and the final profit was
A$(2,681)m, a loss of about 7% of contract value. The paper's "net profit" of (16%) is not the loss
itself: it defines net profit as the difference between final and tendered profit (p.12), so it is
the 16-point swing from a 9% margin to a 7% loss. In the paper's words, each project "destroys its
original 9% profit margin plus a further deterioration of 7%" (abstract).

Individual projects ranged from 1 point better than tendered to 43 points worse.
